Summary

The large numbers are counted from the files. The 63-wallet and 44.65% figures are the owner’s screenshot claim, and they are labeled that way.

Wallets

Watched wallets are the ones the monitor polls. A screenshot match lined up with the owner’s Bubblemaps picture. Other holders are not treated as the cluster.

Funding trail

Each line is the first SOL that arrived in that wallet. The walks stop at exchanges and busy services. No private common funder has been found, and a shared exchange wallet is not the cabal funder.

Holder map

Bubble size is the share of supply. Teal matched the screenshot. Gray is another holder. Amber is an exchange or busy service, not the cabal. Lines are the first SOL in, not proof of one owner. This map is rebuilt every 30 minutes.
